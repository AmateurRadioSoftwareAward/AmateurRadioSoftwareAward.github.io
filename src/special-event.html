Special Event Stations - K7A

We are operating the special event station K7A from Friday, November 27th through Monday, December 7th, 2026 to promote innovative, free and open amateur radio software.
During the event we honor the Hamlib project as the 2026 award recipient with Nate Bargmann (N0NB), George Baltz (N3GB), Daniele Forsi (IU5HKX), and Mikael Nousiainen (OH3BHX) as the current core developers.
As part of the special event we encourage people to submit nominations for the 2027 Amateur Radio Software Award.
